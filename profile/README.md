# Compass — Alumni Portal

Compass is a customizable alumni engagement portal designed to help educational institutions connect, engage, and build lasting relationships with their alumni.

## Features

- Alumni community and networking
- Institution-specific branding and customization
- White-label solutions under your institution's brand
- Custom features and integrations based on requirements
- Flexible deployment options

## Deployment Options

- **Cloud Deployment:** Flexible and scalable hosting solutions.
- **On-Premises Deployment:** Deployment within your institution's infrastructure.
- **Customized Deployment:** Solutions tailored to your technical and operational needs.
